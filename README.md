# Color Lumie

Color Lumie is a private space for you and your closest friends to stay connected through color. We often don't have the words to say how we're feeling, but we always know our vibe. There is no endless scrolling, no public profiles, and no pressure to perform — just a simple way to see who's feeling radiant, who's feeling quiet, and who might need a little extra warmth today. Set your Lumie, share your light, and keep your inner circle close.
